The keys work like the machine's: START/STOP starts this recipe (3 s countdown) or stops the run; EDIT, the arrows, PAUSE (digit sel) and ENTER change the values on the LCD. VACUUM must be switched on at the machine's own keypad, with a substrate on the chuck. Any key on the machine, a lost cable or a vacuum drop also stops a run.
